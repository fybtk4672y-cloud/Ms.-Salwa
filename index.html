My actual conclusion

Dear Ms. Salwa,

One of my favorite things about your Biology class is that you never explain something in only one way.

You give us examples. Then another example. You draw it on the board. You connect the ideas together. You ask us questions. And somehow, by the end, something that could have felt complicated becomes completely crystal clear.

Even when we study topics I am already familiar with, I still genuinely enjoy interacting in your class. I still want to answer, participate, and hear the way you explain it — because you always manage to add another perspective that makes it interesting again.

I especially loved learning about Mendel, genes, heredity, blood types and all the little details around them. I love how you draw things out and make us visualize what is happening instead of simply asking us to memorize information.

But there is something else I appreciate just as much as your explanations: the way you appreciate your students. I really like the feeling of being noticed by you. When I answer or participate, you make it feel like my contribution matters, and that makes me want to participate even more.

Since I came to this school, you have been so helpful to me. I know you're incredibly busy, which makes the time and effort you give us mean even more.

Thank you for making Biology interactive, clear, and genuinely enjoyable. Thank you for every example, every diagram on the board, every question, and every moment you've made me feel seen.

You're an amazing teacher — and such a beautiful person too.

Happy Teacher's Day, Ms. Salwa. 🧬💚

With love and appreciation,
Zoha Salahuddin · 11-P
